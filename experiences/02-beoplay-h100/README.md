# Experience 02 — Beoplay H100 / World Volume

Independent interactive concept for Bang & Olufsen Beoplay H100.

## Technology
- React 19 + Vite
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger
- Native range/pointer/touch interactions
- CSS-driven spatial field and responsive product staging

## Interaction system
- GSAP scroll choreography and pinned World Volume scene
- Interactive ANC ↔ TrueTransparency™ World Volume dial
- Pointer-responsive Spatial Audio field
- Head Tracking mode toggle
- Four real product finish selectors
- Hold-for-Quick-Listen interaction
- Responsive touch behavior and reduced-motion fallback

## Product facts
Factual copy is based on Bang & Olufsen's current Beoplay H100 product/support pages: titanium drivers, Dolby Atmos optimisation, touch surfaces and physical dials, up to 32 hours playback with ANC at moderate volume, up to 30 hours with Spatial Audio + Head Tracking, and Hi-Res USB audio support up to 24-bit / 96 kHz on supported systems.
